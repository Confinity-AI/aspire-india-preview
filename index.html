Why it works

The village makes sure every child learns.every child learns. We make sure the village can.

We get children into school, help them learn well, and hand the running of it to the community — working inside the government system, not around it, until they don't need us.
What we do

Three things, all at once

01

Access

We find children who are out of school and help them get back — and stay to the end.
02

Learning

We help children who have fallen behind catch up, and learn to keep learning on their own.
03

Governance

We help parents, schools, and village councils run and protect education themselves.
